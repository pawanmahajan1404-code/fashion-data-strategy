# Fashion & Apparel Data Strategy

## Overview

A data-driven strategy for the **apparel, textiles, and fashion industry**, focusing on how data science and analytics can improve business decision-making.

## Key Focus Areas

* 📊 Demand & Sales Forecasting
* 📦 Inventory Optimization
* 👥 Customer Analytics
* 📈 Marketing Analytics
* 💰 Pricing & Promotion Analytics
* 🔍 Fashion Trend Analysis
* 🔄 Returns & Product Performance

## Objectives

* Improve demand forecasting and inventory planning
* Identify emerging fashion trends
* Understand customer behavior
* Optimize marketing and pricing decisions
* Enable data-driven business strategies
